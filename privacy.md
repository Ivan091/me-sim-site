# Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

me-sim is a desktop command-line tool that runs entirely on your own computer. This policy explains what it
does with your data.

1. **What it stores.** After you sign in to a platform such as TikTok, the tool stores the access and refresh
   tokens the platform issues in a file in your own user directory. It also keeps a local record of the
   videos it has posted, with their post ids and times, so a video is never posted twice.
2. **What it sends.** The tool sends your videos, their captions and the tokens above to the platform's API
   when you post. It sends nothing to anyone else. There is no server operated by the author, no analytics
   and no tracking.
3. **What it reads.** Before a post the tool asks the platform for your display name and the posting options
   your account allows, shows them to you and discards them. It does not read your followers, messages or
   any other account data.
4. **Deletion.** Delete the token file and the posting record from your computer to remove everything the
   tool holds. Revoke the tool's access in the platform's settings to invalidate the tokens.
5. **Changes.** This policy may change; the date above says when it last did.
